Where the list comes from

Architects: 504 active companies registered as architectural practices in EC1 on Companies House. I removed virtual-office and accountants' addresses (about 375 of them sit at City Road mail-forwarding addresses), then kept about 70 that look like real studios. The address is the registered office. For most studios that is the office itself, but tap the map link to check.

Showrooms: about 150 in the Clerkenwell Showrooms directory, mostly brands showing their own products. Only the 22 that resell, source or run a small brand are listed, after the architects.

What to say at the desk (30 seconds)

Architects and interior designersWe make joinery, kitchens, wardrobes, doors and loose furniture to your drawings in vetted partner factories in China, and deliver to site in the UK with test reports and CE documents. Your design gets built as drawn, at a cost that keeps it in the client's budget. Could we price one item on a current residential or hospitality project? Ask for whoever leads interiors, FF&E or a residential project. At a big practice, ask reception to pass the two-pager to the interiors team.
If they ask about commissionYes, we can pay an introducer fee, agreed in writing and disclosed to the client. Most architects' professional codes require that, and saying it first builds trust.
Dealers and showrooms that resellWhen your clients want something bespoke, or a budget your brands cannot hit, we make it to drawing in our partner factories in China and deliver to site in the UK, with test reports and CE documents before anything is ordered. Could we try it on one item from a live project?
UK brands that make their own rangeWe can second-source parts or a whole line (frames, bases, upholstery, cabinets) with samples and factory checks, so you protect margin without risking quality.
Tile, flooring and finishes distributorsOwn-label runs direct from the factory, with test reports, at container or part-container quantities.
Always ask"Who looks after sourcing or new products?" Get a name and email, leave the one-pager, and email them the same day.

                    Rules for the day

                    1. Architects first: the morning is the best time to catch a partner before site visits. Aim for 15 to 20 doors a day. Showrooms are usually open weekdays 9:00 to 17:30. If one is appointment-only, ask for a name and email and move on.
                    2. Tick each door here, and send me the names and emails you collect (a photo of the business cards is fine). I'll write the follow-up emails the same evening.
                    3. Extend the trip only if, by Thursday evening, at least 3 people have asked for a price, a sample or a meeting.
